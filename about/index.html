Our story

Thirty-five years of Atlanta hospitality.

What began as a catering company became two event halls, then one name. Through all of it, the work has been the same: hosting people well.

  1. 1991

    Dedicaters Inc is founded

    Robert Pope forms Dedicaters Inc, the parent company of Inviting Events. Before founding it, he managed at several five-star hotels and restaurants throughout the Atlanta area.

  2. 2001

    The first event hall

    Dedicaters began as a catering company. In 2001 it opened its first event hall.

  3. 2004

    A second facility

    A second facility opens. The two later merge and become Inviting Events.

  4. Today

    Inviting Events

    Twenty-five years of hosting, for local residents and high-profile celebrities alike, and one of the premier event destinations in the Atlanta metro area.

The owner

Robert Pope. Bob, or Mr. Robert.

Customers call him Bob, or Mr. Robert. He founded Dedicaters Inc in 1991 after managing at several five-star hotels and restaurants throughout the Atlanta area.

Today he walks clients through the rooms himself and builds each event around their vision.
