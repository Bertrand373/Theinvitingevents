About Inviting Events

Inviting Events is owned and run by Robert Pope. He and his staff host events in our three ballrooms in Snellville.

Schedule a tour Read our story

History

  1. 1991

    Dedicaters Inc is founded

    After managing at several five-star hotels and restaurants around Atlanta, Robert Pope forms Dedicaters Inc as a catering company. It is the parent company of Inviting Events.

  2. 2001

    The first event hall

    After ten years of catering, Dedicaters opens its first event hall.

  3. 2004

    A second facility

    A second facility opens. The two later merge as Inviting Events.

  4. Today

    Inviting Events

    Robert still runs the business. Our guests include local families and some well-known names.

Robert Pope, owner

Customers call him Bob, or Mr. Robert. He runs Inviting Events with his sons, Jamal and Elijah.

Call or text him to set up a tour.

Our staff

With the Gold package, our own attendants work your event. Before guests arrive they set the room to your plan, with the table layout, linens, centerpieces and lighting in place. During the event they keep the buffet warm and the tables clear, and when the night is over they break everything down.

The décor comes from two warehouses of our own. Linens, chargers, centerpieces, lighting and larger statement pieces are kept there in a wide range of colors, so we can dress the room for your theme.
