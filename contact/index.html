Questions

How much time do I get for an event?

Bronze and Silver include eight hours, because you handle your own setup, decorating and cleanup. Gold includes six hours, since our staff does the setup and cleanup for you. Wedding ceremonies get two additional hours, and more time can be purchased.

Can I bring my own food?

Yes. You may bring your own food and beverages, including alcohol.

Can I rent a hall without decorations and service?

Yes. With Bronze (room only) or Silver (room with linens), you decorate, set up and clean up yourself. With Gold, our staff takes care of the décor, setup, service and cleanup.

How late can an event last?

All events end no later than 1:00 AM. Additional hours cannot be purchased past that time.

Is there enough parking?

Yes. There is parking on site for your guests.

Can I stop by anytime to see the facility?

The rooms are shown by appointment only. Call Robert at (678) 517-8963 to set a time.

Will I have a choice of linen colors?

Yes. We have two warehouses with your choice of color and décor.
