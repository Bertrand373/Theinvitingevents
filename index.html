The rooms

A room for every size of party.

Each room comes with tables, chairs and lighting. Choose the one that fits your guest count, then decide how much of the décor you want us to handle.

Grand Ballroom

80 to 250 guests

Our largest hall, with a dance floor, connected restrooms and a full ladies' suite with a shower and a makeup station.

Studio 78

50 to 80 guests

A smaller room in a separate building, with its own ladies' suite and a private lounge area.

Auxiliary Ballroom

30 to 60 guests

Next to the Grand Ballroom, with a pool table, a sound system, a 110-inch projector screen and three flat-screen TVs.

Private Lounge & Bar

Up to 60 guests

A flat-rate night in the Auxiliary Ballroom. Six hours, from $1,000.
Everything in house

Décor from our own warehouses.

Linens, chargers, centerpieces, backdrops and lighting all come from our own two warehouses, so there is nothing to rent elsewhere.

Linens

Table linens, runners, napkins and chair sashes in your colors, pulled from our own warehouses.

Table settings

Charger plates, glassware, silverware, candles, mirrors and centerpieces, including tall florals.

Lighting and décor

Stage and party lighting set to your palette, balloon décor, marquee letters, throne chairs and backdrops.

Kitchen and bar

Both come with Gold: the kitchen area with an oven, hot boxes, prep tables and a refrigerator, and the lighted bar with drink dispensers and coffee urns.

Setup and cleanup

With Gold, our attendants set the room, keep the buffet warm and the tables clear, then break it all down. With Bronze or Silver, you handle your own setup, decorating and cleanup.

Preferred vendors

We will put you in touch with a DJ, photographer or bartender we trust.
